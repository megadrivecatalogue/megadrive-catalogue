World Cup Soccer

Sports · SEGA · Mega Drive

Also known as World Cup Soccer ~ World Championship Soccer.

Sega's early football game, also sold as World Championship Soccer.

  • Players: 1–2
  • Regions: JAP, USA
  • Size: 256 KB

Find it on the cartridge

On the MD V3 Pro cartridge, open folder 02 USA Games (N-Z) and scroll to item 378 of 404 (World Cup Soccer ~ World Championship Soccer (Japan, USA) (Rev A).bin).
