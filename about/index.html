About & privacy

About this site

Mega Drive Catalogue is a free, fan-made catalogue of Sega Mega Drive / Genesis, Master System and 32X games: box art, screenshots, short descriptions, release years, developers and number of players. It's a hobby project with no ads and no paywall.

No games are hosted or linked here. The site lists names and facts about the games only. It doesn't offer, link to or explain how to get game files.

Privacy

This site has no accounts, no ads, no tracking and sets no cookies. Fonts are served from this site, not from Google.

Visitor statistics: page views are counted with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly service that uses no cookies and stores no personal data. It records the page, where you came from, browser, screen size and country. Your IP address is used only to work out the country and to avoid counting the same visit twice, and is not stored. Nobody, including me, can identify individual visitors from it.

  • Profiles, favourites, completed games and high scores are saved only in your own browser (its local storage), because you asked the site to remember them. They are never sent to anyone, including me, and aren't used for anything else.
  • Deleting them: delete a profile from the profile menu at the top right, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Private/incognito windows forget them automatically.

Like any website, loading the pages means your browser contacts these services, which can see your IP address and browser details:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) hosts the site, and GitHub serves the game images.
  • GoatCounter counts page views, as described above.

The site is run by a private individual as a non-commercial hobby. Questions about privacy: megadrivecatalogue@gmail.com.

Cookies and browser storage

The site sets no cookies. It keeps only these in your browser, and only for features you use:

  • md-profiles (local storage): your profiles, favourites, completed games and high scores, until you delete them.
  • md-welcome-shown (session storage): remembers that the welcome window was shown, so it doesn't open on every page. It goes when you close the tab.

Neither is used for tracking or sent anywhere, and GoatCounter stores nothing in your browser, so there is no cookie banner.

Your rights and data requests

Visitor statistics are kept on the basis of legitimate interests: knowing which pages are used, without identifying anyone. GoatCounter keeps only totals; your IP address is not stored.

Under UK data protection law you can ask what data is held about you, ask for it to be deleted, or object to its use. The site holds nothing that identifies you, so there is normally nothing to hand over or delete, but any request sent to megadrivecatalogue@gmail.com gets an answer within a month. To remove what your browser keeps, delete your profiles from the profile menu or clear this site's data in your browser settings. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

The site is suitable for all ages. It never asks for personal details: a profile only needs a nickname, and that stays in your browser. If you're under 13 and want to email, ask a parent or carer first.
